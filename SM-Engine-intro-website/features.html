01 toolset map

What the editor can actually do.

A capability map for choosing the right desktop tool: concrete controls, real editor views and the details that make each discipline useful.

01 geometry

Topology, without the ceremony.

Start from a silhouette, then work at vertex, edge or polygon level. The editor keeps precision operations and sculpting in the same modeling pass.

Modelingmesh · sculpt

Topology
Vertex, edge, polygon and loop workflows
Modifiers
Boolean, Mirror, Solidify, Bevel, Subdivision
Sculpt
Clay, smooth, pinch, inflate and crease falloffs

Direct manipulation with live feedback. Every extrusion and bevel answers immediately.

02 systems

Trace every dependency.

Make materials and procedural behavior legible on a node canvas. Typed sockets, grouping and live evaluation show exactly what changes when a value moves.

Node graphsmaterial · logic

Materials
Albedo, roughness, metallic, normals and emission
Logic
Events, variables, math operators and triggers
Feedback
Real-time graph evaluation inside the editor

Cause and effect stays visible. Change one input and read the result without leaving the scene behind.

03 worlds

Author light, not just geometry.

Build a readable atmosphere with physical sky response, volumetric depth and a controllable sun path.

Atmospheresky · light

Sky
Sun, moon and time-of-day control
Atmosphere
Rayleigh, Mie, ozone and depth haze
Lighting
HDR ambient response and volumetric depth

Light defines the reading. A technically correct scene becomes a place with a point of view.

04 simulation

Make environments behave.

Shape land, water and vegetation as responsive systems. Iterate on scale, density and wave response while the scene remains live.

Environmentterrain · water · biome

Ocean
Multi-frequency waves, foam and refraction
Terrain
Heightfield sculpting and large-world shaping
Biomes
GPU-instanced vegetation and density control

Scale becomes legible. Motion in the environment gives the camera a sense of distance and weather.

05 motion

Give timing a point of view.

Block, refine and polish motion with tracks, dope-sheet controls and Bezier curves.

Animationtimeline · rig

Timeline
Multi-track keyframe editing
Rigging
Bone controllers and animation clips
Curves
Bezier tangents for acceleration and easing

Timing turns action into story. Keyframes matter when you can see the rhythm of the shot.

06 post

Make the last pass count.

Bring picture, sound and timing together for review and delivery.

Finishingaudio · video · output

Audio
Channel mixing, EQ and routing
Video
Clip sequencing, transitions and overlays
Output
Direct media rendering workflows

Output keeps the finish honest. Reviewing picture and sound together reveals the last decisions before a scene leaves the editor.
