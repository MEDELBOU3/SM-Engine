Cookies, plainly.

This site sets no cookies of its own. One localStorage key remembers your theme — everything else cookie-shaped comes from embeds you choose to load. Last updated: 13 September 2026.

Switching theme writes one value so the site remembers your choice. No identifiers, no tracking pixels, no banner needed for our own code.

Helpful tip: in your browser, use Site settings → Clear data to remove the theme key at any time.

02 third-party

Embeds may differ.

Pages that load outside services — video players (YouTube, Vimeo), CDN libraries and fonts — can set their own cookies under their own policies. Blocking those domains still leaves docs and articles readable; only the players and CDN animation stop.

Manage cookies anytime in your browser's Privacy → Site settings. For more on our overall approach, see Privacy.
